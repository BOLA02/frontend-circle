# Community Values

## 1. Learn by Building

Reading is important, but building is where learning happens.

---

## 2. Stay Curious

Ask questions.
Experiment.
Break things.
Fix them.

---

## 3. Help Others

If you understand something, teach someone else.

Teaching reinforces learning.

---

## 4. Progress Over Perfection

Nobody starts as an expert.

Small improvements every day lead to big results.

---

## 5. Respect Everyone

We welcome beginners.

No mocking.
No gatekeeping.
No unnecessary criticism.

---

## 6. Consistency Wins

Showing up every week is better than studying for ten hours once a month.
